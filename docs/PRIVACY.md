# Ocean: Study & Focus Privacy Policy

Ocean is a study planner and focus timer for iPhone and Apple Watch. The developer does not collect data through the app. Ocean has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## Study data

Ocean stores the study information you enter and the records you create, including tasks, schedules, focus sessions, and settings, on your device. When you use a paired Apple Watch, Ocean shares the information needed to show and control your study sessions across your paired devices. The developer does not receive this information. Your device's system backups follow your Apple settings.

## Notifications, backups, and external links

If you allow notifications, Ocean schedules them on your device. You can change notification permission in your device settings. If you export a JSON backup, you choose where it goes. Exported files may contain your study records. If you open an external link, the destination handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

## Deleting data and contacting support

You can delete individual records in Ocean or remove the app and its data from your device. You manage any exported copies separately. If you email support, your message and return email address are used to respond to your request.

Privacy questions can be sent to [slowforge.studio@gmail.com](mailto:slowforge.studio@gmail.com).
