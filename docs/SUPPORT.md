# Ocean: Study & Focus Support

For help with Ocean on iPhone or Apple Watch, email [slowforge.studio@gmail.com](mailto:slowforge.studio@gmail.com). Include your Ocean version, device model, OS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Leave private study records and backup files out of your initial message.

## Getting started

- **Today** shows your study tasks and starts a focus session.
- **Flow** shows your study blocks in the week or month calendar.
- **Ocean** summarizes your saved focus time and activity.
- **Settings** contains appearance, study availability, notifications, and backup controls.

For Apple Watch, open Ocean on the paired iPhone first, then open Ocean on the watch. If the app is missing from the watch, open the Watch app on iPhone and look under Available Apps.
